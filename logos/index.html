01

Interlock My pick

Two square frames linked like chain, one bronze and one espresso, taken from the interlocking frames of the sculpture in the Sculpted Ivory hero.

What it says

Websites, booking and automation are separate systems that you join so they work as one.

Small sizes

Strong at 32px and up. At 16px the link reads as a solid mark.

Hallmark

A round hallmark with an italic serif A at the centre and the name and services stamped around the edge.

Recommendation

Go with Interlock

It is the only mark that comes straight out of the Sculpted Ivory sculpture, so the logo and a 3D hero can be the same object. It also says what the studio does: separate systems linked into one.

Pair it with the Bronze Tittle wordmark for editorial moments such as proposals, decks and the site footer. Keep Hallmark for social avatars if you want a round badge there.

Every logo is a real vector file with the lettering converted to outlines, so it prints sharp at any size and needs no fonts installed. Instrument Serif and Manrope are free for commercial use under the SIL Open Font License. Before you register the name or logo, run a UK IPO trade-mark search for "Avantix".
